Archimedis Opera omnia cum commentariis Eutocii, edited by J. L. Heiberg,

Public-domain printed text and images. Full pages rendered by Poppler.
This is the pre-palimpsest edition: its Latin proof of II.2 breaks off,
and II.4 omits a denominator that Heiberg supplies in a footnote.
The retained Latin excerpts follow the body text, including that omission.
